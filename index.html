Questions owners ask

Who pays for the job ads?

We do. During the pilot, ad spend, screening and reference checks are all on us. You only pay the flat fee for each qualified candidate we book onto your calendar.

What counts as a qualified candidate?

Someone who meets our standards for availability, transportation, experience and references, and shows the patience, kindness and care it takes to work with older adults. They pass our phone screen and book an interview with you. We share the full definition in writing before your first invoice.

Is there a long contract?

No. It's month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Which areas do you serve?

We're starting with non-medical home care agencies in Illinois. Everything runs remotely, so we can recruit in any metro you serve.

Do you replace my office staff?

No. We take the chasing and screening off their plate, so they can spend their time on interviews, onboarding and clients.
