Part-Time Sales Representative

Remote, US 10 to 15 hrs/week $18 to $24/hr + commission

Keen Care is hiring a part-time sales representative to team up with our CEO. Keen Care provides the prospect list. Your main goal is to set up meetings between decision makers at home care agencies and HR teams and our CEO. The CEO runs the sales conversation and closes the deal, so you and the CEO work as one team.

Apply now

Responsibilities

  1. Work from the prospect list Keen Care provides, calling decision makers at home care agencies and HR teams.
  2. Reach the decision maker, learn about their caregiver staffing needs, and earn a meeting with our CEO.
  3. Follow up by phone, email, and voicemail until a meeting is booked or the prospect is ruled out.
  4. Document the process: call notes, what works, objections, and a clear playbook the team can reuse.
  5. Hand off each booked meeting with a short brief for the CEO.
  6. Suggest improvements to outreach, messaging, and the prospect list based on what you learn.

Requirements

  • Self-starter who manages your own time and follows through without daily check-ins.
  • Hungry: persistent, competitive, and motivated by results.
  • Kind: warm and respectful with every prospect, including the ones who say no.
  • Experienced in phone outreach, sales development, or engaging HR or home care contacts.
  • Organized, with clear notes and follow-ups.
  • Comfortable documenting processes so others can repeat what works.
  • Available for weekday calling blocks in Mountain Time.
  • Able to work legally in the United States.
